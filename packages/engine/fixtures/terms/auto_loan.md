# Lone Star Auto Finance: Auto Loan Terms (account ending 4417)

## Payment and due date
Your monthly payment of $400.00 is due on the 3rd of each month and is collected by autopay from your checking account. A payment that falls on a weekend or holiday posts on the next business day.

## Changing your due date
You may ask to move your monthly due date once every 12 months. A customer service representative can approve a new due date anywhere from the 1st to the 28th of the month. A due date change carries a processing fee of up to $35.00. The fee may be reduced or waived at the representative's discretion. The change takes effect with the next scheduled payment, and interest accrues daily, so a later due date adds a small amount of interest to that month.

## Grace period and late fees
A payment received within 10 days after the due date is not considered late. After the grace period, a late fee of $25.00 is charged once per late payment. Late payments of 30 days or more may be reported to the credit bureaus.

## Interest
The loan carries a fixed annual percentage rate of 6.9%. Interest is calculated daily on the unpaid principal. Extra payments go to principal and there is no prepayment penalty.

## Verification
For your security, representatives may only discuss or change the account after verifying the account holder's identity. If a caller cannot be verified, the representative will take a callback number and the account holder will be contacted directly.

## Deferrals
Payment deferrals (skipping a payment) are not offered on this loan except in documented hardship cases, which require a written application.
