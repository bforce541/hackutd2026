# Bluebonnet Card: Cardholder Terms (account ending 3304)

## Payments and due date
The minimum payment is due on the 12th of each month. Your autopay pays $750.00 from checking each month on the due date. You may change your due date once per billing year to any day from the 1st to the 28th; there is no fee for this change, and it takes effect on the next statement.

## Grace period
Purchases have a grace period of 25 days when the previous statement balance was paid in full. When a balance is carried, interest accrues from the purchase date.

## Late fees and penalty rate
A late fee of up to $30.00 is charged when the minimum payment is not received by the due date, and up to $41.00 if a payment was also late within the previous six billing cycles. A payment 60 days or more past due can trigger the penalty APR of 29.99%.

## Interest rate
The purchase APR is 24.99% variable. Interest is calculated on the average daily balance. Paying the full statement balance avoids interest on new purchases.

## Credit limit
Your credit limit is $5,000.00. Charges that would exceed the limit may be declined. Over-limit fees are not charged.

## Payment methods
Payments can be made by autopay, online, by phone, or by mail. Payments made by phone before 5:00 p.m. Central Time post the same day.
