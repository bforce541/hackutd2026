# Prairie Home Lending: Mortgage Servicing Terms (account ending 5529)

## Payment and due date
Your monthly mortgage payment of $1,200.00 is due on the 1st of the month and collected by autopay on the first business day. It includes principal, interest and escrow for taxes and insurance.

## Changing your due date
The mortgage due date is set when the loan is originated and cannot be moved by phone. A one-time change is available only through a written hardship or loan modification request, which can take 30 days or more to review.

## Grace period and late fees
There is a 15-day grace period. After it, a late fee of 5% of the overdue principal and interest payment is charged. Payments 30 days late may be reported to the credit bureaus.

## Escrow
Taxes and homeowner's insurance are paid from escrow. The escrow account is reviewed once a year, and your payment may change after the review.

## Extra payments
Extra principal payments are accepted at any time with no penalty. Mark the payment as "principal only" so it does not count as an early monthly payment.

## Hardship help
If you expect to miss a payment, contact the servicer before the due date to discuss forbearance or a repayment plan. Interest continues to accrue during forbearance.
