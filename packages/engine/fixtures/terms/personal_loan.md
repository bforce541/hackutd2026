# Cedar Personal Loans: Loan Terms (account ending 6182)

## Payment and due date
Your monthly payment of $150.00 is due on the 23rd of each month. You can pay by autopay, online or by phone.

## Changing your due date
You may request a new due date once per loan term. Requests can be made by phone and are usually approved on the same call. The new date must fall between the 1st and the 28th. There is no fee for the first change; any further change carries a $15.00 fee.

## Grace period and late fees
There is a 5-day grace period. After it, a late fee of $15.00 or 5% of the payment, whichever is greater, is charged once per late payment.

## Interest
The loan carries a fixed annual percentage rate of 11.9%. There is no prepayment penalty, and extra payments reduce principal.

## Verification
Representatives must verify the account holder before making changes. If the caller cannot be verified, no changes are made and the representative will note the request.
